Before you start: introduce one new food at a time (waiting a couple of days between new allergens like egg, dairy, peanut, tree nuts, soy, wheat, fish, and shellfish) and watch for any reaction. Skip honey and unpasteurized foods before age one, and avoid choking hazards like whole nuts, whole grapes, or hard raw pieces — serve mashed, thinly sliced, or ground instead. When in doubt, check with Jaxon's pediatrician.
